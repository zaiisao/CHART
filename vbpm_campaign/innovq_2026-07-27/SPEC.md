## 0. Premise (established by the rebuild campaign; not re-litigated)

The fixed objective (physical prior γ_phase=5.5e-4 ⇒ ρ_p=0.999450, Student-t(2, 0.00125)
log-tempo level increments, latent-only decoders, β=1) HOLDS truth: a free (SVI) q stays at
corr 0.634→0.635, F 0.758→0.762. Every amortized per-frame-absolute-phase cell collapses at
handover (10/10; corr→0.002, klph≈5,800–6,000/crop in the m2 logs) because the encoder emits
trajectories whose one-step innovations (0.063 rad) are ~2,000x larger than the dynamics
allow (truth 3.1e-5 rad), and the sharp prior correctly prices that jitter at 483–589
nats/crop against ~108 nats of phase recon value. **Fix: make smoothness structural.** The
posterior's samples are generated by the prior's own recursion plus small encoder-emitted
innovations; then q's trajectories satisfy the physics by construction and the KL charges
only the innovations.

## (a) The posterior

Encoder trunk unchanged: the existing `post_gru` (bi-GRU over `[h, b]`) → `post_ctx`
(128-d per-frame context `c_t`), exactly as `BarPointerVAE.encode_posterior`. New heads
replace the absolute-state `post_head`:

**(i) t=1 initial state** (one head on pooled context `mean_t c_t`, plus `c_1`):
- `q(φ_1) = WC(μ_φ1, ρ_1)` with `ρ_1 = sigmoid(·) * ρ1_max`, **ρ1_max = 0.9** (generous
  width mandated — this is the error-correction reservoir, see (f));
- `q(ℓ_1) = Student-t(ν_p, μ_ℓ1 + level_offset, s_ℓ1)`, `s_ℓ1` floored at 0.05 (prior init
  scale 0.3);
- `q(m) = Cat(softmax(meter_logits))` — one draw per crop, redrawn at bar crossings from the
  same logits (exactly the pm_common convention).

**(ii) Per-frame innovation distributions**, head `innov_head([c_t, z_feat(z_{t-1})])`
(same input signature as the old `post_head`, so the m2 harness maps over):
- phase: `ε^φ_t ~ WC(μ^φ_t, ρ_q_t)` with `μ^φ_t = tanh(u_t) · s_φ`, **s_φ = 0.05 rad**
  (bound; anneal 0.05→0.02 after placement if saturation diagnostics allow), and
  `ρ_q_t = 1 - softplus(r_t + r0)` initialized at ρ_p (bias r0 set so ρ_q≈ρ_p at init);
- tempo (level): `ε^ℓ_t ~ Student-t(ν_p=2, μ^ℓ_t, s^ℓ_t)` with `μ^ℓ_t = tanh(v_t) · s_ℓ`,
  **s_ℓ = 0.0025 = 2σ_Δℓ** and `s^ℓ_t` init at σ_Δℓ=0.00125;
- dev: pinned at the physical floor (dev_ar=0, σ=1e-3), as in the fixed objective;
- meter: as in pm_common (crossing-gated redraw, KL charged at crossings only).

**Trajectory construction (q's sampler = the prior's recursion + nudges):**
```
φ_t = wrap( φ_{t-1} + exp(ℓ_{t-1}) + ε^φ_t )        ε^φ_t ~ WC(μ^φ_t, ρ_q_t)   [inverse-CDF reparam]
ℓ_t =        ℓ_{t-1}               + ε^ℓ_t          ε^ℓ_t ~ St-t rsample
```
Equivalently: q(z_t | z_{t-1}, x, b) is wrapped-Cauchy centered at the **prior transition
mean computed from the sampled z_{t-1}**, shifted by the encoder nudge. Same distributional
family per-coordinate as the spec's q; only the mean parameterization changed (see (g)).

## (b) The KL — reduction to innovation space, with toy verification

Both q and p factor autoregressively over the same graph, so
`KL(q‖p) = E_q Σ_t KL( q(z_t|z_{t-1}) ‖ p(z_t|z_{t-1}) )` evaluated at q's sampled z_{t-1}.
For phase, both conditionals are WC with means `pred_t + μ^φ_t` and `pred_t` where
`pred_t = φ_{t-1}+exp(ℓ_{t-1})` is the **same** quantity (same sampled prev state). The
closed form (Poisson-kernel identity, `vbpm.distributions.kl_wrapped_cauchy`) depends on the
means only through `cos(μ_q-μ_p) = cos(μ^φ_t)`, hence per step, **sample-path independent**:

    KL_t = log[ (1 - 2ρ_qρ_p cos μ^φ_t + (ρ_qρ_p)²) / ((1-ρ_q²)(1-ρ_p²)) ]
         ≈ log[ 1 + (μ^φ_t / 2γ_p)² ]   for ρ_q=ρ_p, small μ    → 0 as μ^φ_t→0.

Tempo likewise: `KL(St-t(ν,μ^ℓ_t,s_q) ‖ St-t(ν,0,σ_Δℓ))`, single-sample MC via the certified
`pm_common.kl_t_mc` (no closed form needed). **t=1 KL** (stated): phase vs near-uniform
physical init `WC(π,1e-6)` = `-log(1-ρ_1²)` + O(ρ_p·1e-6) = **1.66 nats at ρ_1=0.9**
(0.29 at ρ_1=0.5); meter ≤ log 4 = 1.39; level-init MC term O(1) at the widths above.

## (c) Gradient path

Full BPTT through the 256-step integration; **no detach anywhere inside q's recursion** —
the trajectory must be differentiable in (init state, innovations); that is the entire point.
The phase recursion is a pure integrator (∂φ_t/∂ε^φ_k = 1 ∀k≤t — no vanishing/exploding);
the tempo→phase coupling accumulates ∂φ_t/∂ε^ℓ_k = Σ_{j=k}^{t-1} exp(ℓ_j) ≈ 0.06·(t-k) ≤ 16
per crop — bounded. Heavy-tail risk is the WC inverse-CDF reparam `tan(π(u-1/2))`: clamp
u∈[1e-4, 1-1e-4] (bounds |ε| ≤ γ_q·tan(π(0.5-1e-4)) ≈ 3183γ_q ≈ 1.75 rad at ρ_q=ρ_p) +
the existing grad-clip 5.0. Memory: B=16×T=256×(z_feat 7 + heads 128) activations ≈ a few
MB — negligible. Speed: same order as the m2 ELBO loop (0.4–0.5 it/s incl. probes on this
host); placement stage identical to m2 stage 1.

## (f) Failure modes + fallback

1. **Rigidity** (cannot correct a large early phase error): mitigations = generous t=1 width
   (ρ1_max=0.9 → the init KL is only 1.66 nats — cheap insurance), s_φ=0.05 allows 0.05
   rad/frame ≈ full-bar correction in ~2.5 s, and (v2 lever, only if saturation diagnostics
   fire) confidence-gated widening: let ρ_q_t drop where the encoder flags low confidence —
   width, unlike mean jitter, is priced gently (KL(ρ_q=0.9995 vs ρ_p, μ=0) = 0.21/frame).
2. **Vanishing/exploding BPTT**: analyzed in (c) — integrator Jacobian is 1; tempo coupling
   bounded; residual risk is Cauchy tail draws → u-clamp + grad-clip.
3. **Teacher jitter absorption** (placement forces innovations to copy PF noise): solved
   upstream by the w=101 smoothing (executed: corr preserved, innovations 67x smaller).
4. **Slow drift** (many tiny same-sign nudges = tempo error laundering through phase): watch
   the μ^φ sign-autocorrelation diagnostic; if fired, tighten s_φ anneal and let ε^ℓ carry it.
5. **Named fallback**: **semi-amortized VI** — K SVI refinement steps (the certified free-q
   machinery, `elbo_vec`) initialized at the amortized rollout, gradients through or truncated
   (SVI held truth: corr 0.634→0.635, ELBO 991) — if pure amortization still erodes.

## (g) Faithfulness memo + deviation ledger

**Why this stays inside the tutorial's variational family.** The tutorial specifies
q_φ(z|x,b): amortized, over the three latents (bar phase, log-tempo, meter). It specifies
the FAMILY, not the parameterization of q's means. Our q is still amortized (all parameters
emitted by the encoder from (x,b)), still autoregressive-normalized over the same latents,
and each one-step conditional is the same distribution type used throughout the campaign's
certified ELBO (wrapped-Cauchy phase / Student-t level / categorical meter). Structured and
autoregressive posteriors are standard practice in the DVAE literature (e.g. deep Kalman
filters and DVAE-family posteriors that condition z_t on z_{t-1}; the tutorial's own
physics-first stance argues the dynamics-conjugate posterior is the *natural* member of the
family). The ELBO is the plain single-sample SGVB estimator at β=1; the KL accounting is the
SAME certified estimator (V1: rel diff 2.3e-4), merely evaluated at a q whose conditional
means track the sampled previous state. Restricting q (tanh-bounded innovation means) only
tightens the bound direction — it can never falsify the model; it is a capacity choice
inside the family.
